robots.txt is used to tell web crawlers which parts of a website they are allowed or not allowed to access, helping protect server resources and sensitive content. It prevents aggressive or malicious scraping that could overload systems or expose internal pages not meant for indexing. In this way, it promotes ethical scraping by setting clear boundaries so bots can behave responsibly and respect site owners’ rules.
